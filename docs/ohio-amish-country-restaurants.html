Holmes County and Geauga County

Ohio's Amish Country centers on Holmes County (Berlin, Walnut Creek, Sugarcreek) and its neighbors, with a second large settlement around Middlefield in Geauga County. The restaurants on this list cook in the homestyle way the region is known for, and several keep a bakery by the door. They're listed for the cooking, not for who owns them.

The list at a glance

Towns with the most: Berlin (2), Wilmot (1), Walnut Creek (1), Sugarcreek (1), Middlefield (1).

Our method: a place made the list only if a page names it for this food (a local news story, Ohio Magazine, a tourism board or the place's own site) and something from 2025 or 2026 shows it's open: a dated page, the place's own site with its current hours when we looked, or a 2025–26 Cincinnati inspection. Its street address had to match our records. Review sites played no part, and places run by region and town rather than by anybody's score. Hours change, so check before you go.

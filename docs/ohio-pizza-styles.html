Ohio pizza styles

15 pizzerias for the pizza Ohio made its own: Columbus's thin crust, Dayton's thin, square-cut pies, Ohio Valley squares and Youngstown's Brier Hill style, each checked in fall 2026 against a 2025–26 source.

Cut in squares

Most Ohio pizzerias cut a round pie into small squares, and several cities have a style of their own. Columbus-style is a thin, crisp crust with the toppings spread right to the edge. Dayton-style, from Marion's Piazza (1965), is thin and square-cut too. Ohio Valley-style, from Steubenville and the river towns, is baked in a rectangular pan and topped with cold cheese and toppings after it leaves the oven. Youngstown's Brier Hill pizza is a thicker crust under red sauce, bell peppers and Romano.

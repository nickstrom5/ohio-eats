# ohio-eats

Three things built from one pipeline (ported from `../mi-eats/`, with pieces from `../co-eats/`; those folders are never modified):

1. **The web leaderboard**: Ohio restaurants ranked on several boards, published as a private claude.ai Artifact from `site/`. It uses the
   UCSD Google Local 2021 snapshot for ratings, labeled "2021" everywhere, so `site/` and its generator never go in a public repo.
2. **Ohio Eats: Restaurants**, a free iPhone and iPad app (`OhioEats/`, XcodeGen `project.yml`). No Google-derived data: places come
   from Overture Maps open data and the Cincinnati and Columbus health departments' lists; ratings and hours come live from Apple Maps'
   own place card.
3. **The website** in `docs/` (generated by `scripts/make-site.py`): landing page, the hand-checked guides (Cincinnati chili, Ohio pizza
   styles, Amish Country), the James Beard honorees, city pages, what Cincinnati's and Columbus's inspection records mean, the web app at
   `/explore/`, privacy and terms. QA with `scripts/qa-site.py`.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the rules and commands, and `playbook/` for sources and calibration (`12-sources.md`) and the naming round
(`05-naming.md`).

## Official records
- **Cincinnati Health Department**, Cincinnati Food Safety Program (data.cincinnati-oh.gov, public domain): live food licenses and each
  visit's own result since the City's 2024 records system ("Approved - No Violations", "Approved - Minor Violations", "Approved -
  Violations Abated", "Not In Compliance"), shown word for word with the violations cited. No grade is computed.
- **Columbus Public Health**, Restaurant & Market Health Inspections (CC0): every food permit and the City's own status ("Standards
  Met", "Under Enforcement", "Closed for Violation"). No dates or counts are published in bulk.
- Ohio has no statewide list of food licenses and no statewide inspection data; elsewhere, health districts publish one search at a time.
- **James Beard Foundation** honors and America's Classics, as facts. Ohio has no MICHELIN Guide.

## Hand-checked research
`pipeline/build_research.py` writes `data/research/` (private): Cincinnati chili, goetta, pierogi and Cleveland plates, Ohio pizza styles,
Barberton chicken, Toledo's Hungarian hot dogs, Lake Erie perch and walleye, Amish Country and the oldest places, each with a page naming
the place for the food and 2025–26 evidence that it's open.
